Kapoor Beauty Spa

A complete brand identity for a local beauty spa — from logo concept through to a printed grand opening flyer — built around a sense of calm, care, and quiet luxury.

RoleLogo & Print Design
ToolsIllustrator, Photoshop
Year2026
01

The Brief

What the project set out to do, and the story behind the brand.

Process

Kapoor Beauty Spa needed a brand that felt warm and welcoming without slipping into generic spa clichés. The work began with logo sketching — exploring a range of directions from stacked stones and lotus flowers to a refined face silhouette with botanical leaves. After developing multiple logo variations across colour palettes and lockup styles, the face-and-leaf mark emerged as the strongest direction: elegant, feminine, and immediately communicating the brand's focus on personal care. The colour palette — deep forest green and warm cream — was chosen to feel premium but approachable. With the logo resolved, the identity was extended into a grand opening flyer that balanced promotional clarity with the brand's calm visual tone.

Challenge

The main challenge was finding a logo direction that felt distinctive rather than generic — beauty spa logos often default to the same handful of symbols. Working through six sketch concepts and then many digital variations allowed the strongest direction to emerge naturally rather than being forced. The flyer then had to carry promotional urgency (a grand opening, a discount offer, a date and time) while still feeling consistent with a brand built on calm and care — a balance that required careful hierarchy and restraint with decorative elements.

Outcome

The final brand identity gives Kapoor Beauty Spa a consistent, polished visual presence across logo and print. The face-and-leaf mark is clean enough to work at small sizes but detailed enough to hold interest at scale. The grand opening flyer communicates all key information clearly while feeling like it belongs to the brand — not just a generic promotional template.
