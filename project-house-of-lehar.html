House of Lehar

A luxury South Asian fashion brand identity and e-commerce website — spanning information architecture and user flow through wireframes, to a fully designed mobile and desktop digital experience.

RoleBrand Identity & Web Design
ToolsIllustrator, Figma, InDesign
Year2026
01

The Brief

What the project set out to do, and the story behind the brand.

Process

House of Lehar began with research into the South Asian luxury fashion space and how cultural identity, craft, and contemporary style intersect online. The work moved through a full UX process — site mapping, user flow diagrams, and mobile wireframes — before moving into high-fidelity visual design across both mobile and desktop, built in Figma with the brand identity developed in Illustrator.

Challenge

The central challenge was designing something that felt genuinely luxurious and culturally rooted without leaning on tired South Asian design clichés. Every decision — typeface, colour palette, navigation structure, product layout — had to feel considered and elevated while remaining warm and easy to shop. Translating that refinement consistently across mobile and desktop without losing its character was the key design problem to solve.

Outcome

The final brand identity and web design present House of Lehar as a brand that balances cultural pride with contemporary luxury — a visual world that feels at home alongside international fashion houses while remaining distinctly South Asian at its core.
